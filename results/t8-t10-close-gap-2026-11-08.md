## Candidate explanations (ranked; evidence from the tables above)

1. **Winner-side composition shift that the model's yes-rate did not track.** Close
   divisions are won by "yes" far more often in T10 (31.7%) than in T8 (15.2%) — and
   post-cutoff even more (34.1%). The model's pred-yes share rose only 14.0% → 20.2%
   while the actual share rose 15.2% → 31.7%; the shortfall (11.5 pp) is essentially
   the whole close-division gap, and the error mass is one-sided: false-no dominates
   (1.2% → 13.4%) while false-yes is ≈ 0 in T8 and 1.9% in T10. Caveat: T9 had an even
   higher yes-share (37.7%) and scored 0.9871 — the share alone does not determine
   accuracy (see #3).
2. **The T10 close collapse deepens after the §9 cutoff.** Within T10, close accuracy
   drops 0.8688 (pre-2026) → 0.8099 (post-2026), −5.9 pp — much larger than the −1.7 pp
   overall pre/post gap, so the close bucket carries most of the within-term temporal
   signal. The model's pred-yes share *falls* post-cutoff (0.208 → 0.192) while the
   yes-win share rises (0.304 → 0.341). Descriptive only: three cutoff points, no CIs,
   `declared_cutoff` unknown — a control observation, not a contamination finding.
3. **Vote-type mix is not the driver.** The gap persists within every major vote type
   (bill_whole 0.953 → 0.661, resolution_whole 1.000 → 0.696, appointment 1.000 → 0.714,
   wotum 1.000 → 0.636); only `first_reading_rejection` is nearly flat (0.923 → 0.890) —
   the one type whose outcome is structurally one-sided (rejection motions almost
   always fail). The described subset is small (T8 258, T10 300) and conditional on
   description availability.
4. **The measured room-structure axes are flat.** n_active (422/424), club tracks (6/7),
   polarization (0.908/0.904), inni share (0.012/0.018), votings per sitting (25.8/23.9) —
   none of the composition axes measured here moves between T8 and T10, so the gap must
   live in something these artifacts do not capture (party-system alignment structure,
   agenda selection, or a training-data era effect).
5. **Margin-band composition contributes but does not explain.** T10 close divisions
   concentrate in higher bands (36–50: 1,021 vs T8 161) and the gap is largest at
   11–20/21–35 (−0.28/−0.22) — but even the 36–50 band shows −0.09, and T10 outperforms
   T8 at 6–10 (+0.17).
